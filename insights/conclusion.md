
# Final Conclusion - Netflix Data Analysis

## Dataset Overview

The dataset contains 8,807 Netflix titles.

## Main Findings

1. Content Distribution

Movies account for 69.62% of the dataset,
while TV Shows account for 30.38%.

2. Release Year

The most represented release year is 2018,
with 1,147 titles.

3. Country Distribution

United States has the highest number of
country-title associations (3,689).

4. Content Ratings

The most frequently recorded content rating
is TV-MA, with 3,207 titles.

5. Genre Distribution

The most frequently listed genre is International Movies,
appearing in 2,752 title-genre associations.

## Overall Conclusion

The analysis provides an overview of Netflix's
content catalog based on content types, release years,
country associations, ratings, and genres.

The results demonstrate how data analysis and
visualization can help identify patterns and
communicate findings from structured datasets.

## Limitations

This dataset does not contain streaming views,
watch time, revenue, or customer engagement data.

Therefore, the analysis describes catalog characteristics
rather than actual audience preferences or business performance.
